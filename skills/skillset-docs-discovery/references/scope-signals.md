# Scope Signals

Count rounds, not questions. A normal session closes in about three to five rounds.

## 1. Scope too large

- Signs: frontier keeps growing, branches multiply, answers add more branches than they close.
- Fix: pause, split the effort into smaller pieces, grill one piece per session.

## 2. Context overload

- Signs: later questions get vaguer, repeat earlier ground, or ignore prior answers.
- Fix: close early with what is decided, record open branches explicitly, start fresh for the remainder.

## 3. Done

- The frontier is empty: every branch visited, nothing left silently assumed.
- Checks: the user disagreed at least once, landed somewhere unexpected, and can defend each choice without the transcript.
- Close by listing decisions plus open items. Persist to spec or ADR only with explicit approval.

## 4. Stuck

- The user cannot answer after evidence and throwaway options are exhausted.
- Mark it open with owner and next evidence step. Do not guess on their behalf.
